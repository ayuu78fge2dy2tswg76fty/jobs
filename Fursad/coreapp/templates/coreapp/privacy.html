Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fursad. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our job portal.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you:

  • Register as a Jobseeker or Company.
  • Upload your CV or profile details.
  • Post job openings or apply for jobs.
  • Communicate with us via our contact forms.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide our core services, including connecting candidates with employers, improving our platform, and ensuring a safe recruitment environment.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. and every one can delete their account at any time.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with employers (when you apply) or as required by law.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@fursad.so.
